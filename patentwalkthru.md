## 🗣️ **Speaking Script: My Patent Filing Journey (30 mins)**

---

### 🎯 **Intro – 2 mins**

"Good morning/afternoon everyone.
Today, I’m excited to share my personal journey and learning experience around filing two patents. This is not just about the outcome, but the process—how an idea evolved into a patentable innovation.

I’ll walk you through how we formed the group, generated and refined ideas, used tools like ChatGPT to accelerate our thinking, and how we validated and submitted the final outcomes."

---

### 👥 **1. Team Formation & Idea Generation – 4 mins**

"The journey started when a small innovation group was formed in our team in the month of **November 2024**.
We were around 4 to 5 members, and we had a dedicated co-ordinator who guided us through the patent process.

Our first step was **idea generation**. Each of us was asked to come up with **three to four different ideas** based on our individual areas—be it **technology**, **process improvement**, or **security innovation**.

So overall, we had **15 raw ideas** to start with. These ideas weren’t polished yet, and we didn’t filter them. The goal was to just get as many diverse, innovative thoughts on the table as possible."

---

### 🗳️ **2. Idea Ranking & Shortlisting – 3 mins**

"Once we had all 15 ideas, we moved into a collaborative ranking process. The entire group reviewed and discussed each idea. We looked at factors like:

* Uniqueness
* Technical depth
* Potential impact
* Feasibility within our domain

After this internal evaluation, we selected the **top 3 ideas** for further exploration. These three stood out as being strong candidates for patent filing.

That selection was important because it allowed us to focus and go deep, rather than spreading ourselves too thin across 15 ideas."

---

### 🧠 **3. Daily Brainstorming Sessions – 4 mins**

"We then moved into a focused refinement phase. We scheduled daily sync-ups—short sessions of about 10 to 20 minutes—where we brainstormed as a group.

During these syncs, we would:

* Expand on each idea’s scope
* Challenge the feasibility
* Debate use cases and edge cases
* Explore different tech stack options to bring novelty

It was collaborative and iterative. Every day, the idea evolved just a little more, and that momentum made a big difference."

---

### 🤖 **4. Using ChatGPT as a Thinking Partner – 3 mins**

"One thing that really helped us during this phase was ChatGPT.

We used it not just for research, but for **creative ideation**. For example:

* If we had a problem, we asked: *'What’s an alternate way to solve this using X technology?'*
* It helped us articulate edge cases and workflows clearly
* We even used it to validate whether our idea already existed in public space
* Sometimes, it suggested variations or gaps that we hadn’t even considered

It became a 24/7 brainstorming partner. It saved time, brought fresh angles, and helped sharpen our positioning."

---

### 🔍 **5. Validating Patentability – 4 mins**

"Once we had a fairly shaped idea, our next step was to validate whether it was truly patentable.

This involved three key questions:

1. Is it **novel**? Has this been done before?
2. Is it **non-obvious**? Would a typical developer or architect easily think of this?
3. Is it **useful** in a real-world scenario?

We did a light **prior art check**—searching existing patents on platforms like Google Patents, reviewing known open-source tools, and internal project references.

That gave us confidence that we weren’t reinventing the wheel, and that our approach had defensible innovation."

---

### 🎓 **6. Key Learnings & Takeaways – 4 mins**

"Looking back, a few things made this journey successful:

* Having a small, focused group kept the momentum alive
* Daily micro-brainstorming helped evolve the idea continuously
* Being open to reshaping or dropping ideas when needed
* Using ChatGPT made ideation and validation significantly faster
* And most importantly — collaboration. Innovation is never solo. Everyone brought different perspectives to the table."

---

### ✅ **7. Where We Are Now – 1 min**

"Today, we’ve submitted two patent applications and are awaiting updates.
We're also confident and better prepared for more rounds in the future, having built a process that works."

---

### ❓ **8. Wrap Up & Q\&A – 1 min**

"That was my journey—from forming a team, to building an idea, to filing patents.
Thanks for listening, and I’d be happy to take any questions you might have about the process, tools we used, or lessons learned."
