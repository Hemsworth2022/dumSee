### **Explanation of the Flow Diagram with Decision-Making**

1. **Start**:
   - The customer initiates the process by requesting the status of their data purging through the Generative AI chatbot interface.

2. **Generative AI Chatbot Interface**:
   - The chatbot interacts with the customer using NLP to understand the request and gather necessary information (e.g., PTYID, SSN, TIN, DOB).

3. **Reinforcement Learning (RL) Authentication**:
   - The RL model verifies the customer's identity using the provided information.
   - **Decision Point**: Is the customer authenticated?
     - **No**: Inform the customer that they have no relationship with the bank.
     - **Yes**: Proceed to the next step.

4. **Deep Learning Model (Data Purging Verification)**:
   - The Deep Learning model checks the bank's systems to determine if the customer's data has been purged.
   - **Decision Point**: Is the data already purged?
     - **No**: Create a back-office request for data purging and notify the customer.
     - **Yes**: Proceed to the next step.

5. **Blockchain Audit Trail**:
   - The system logs the data purging status and customer request on a blockchain ledger for transparency and immutability.

6. **Real-Time Confirmation**:
   - The Generative AI chatbot generates a real-time confirmation message for the customer.

7. **Notify Customer**:
   - The customer is notified of the data purging status (either confirmed or pending).

8. **End**:
   - The process concludes.

### **Key Decision Points**

1. **Customer Authentication**:
   - If the customer cannot be authenticated, the process terminates, and the customer is informed.

2. **Data Purging Status**:
   - If the data has not been purged, a back-office request is created, and the customer is notified of the pending action.

3. **Real-Time Confirmation**:
   - If the data has been purged, the customer receives immediate confirmation.
